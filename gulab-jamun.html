Gulab Jamun

Dessert · Soft fried milk dumplings soaked in rose-cardamom syrup

Instructions

  1. Make the syrup: boil the sugar, water and cardamom for 8 to 10 minutes until slightly sticky. Add the saffron, rose water and lemon juice. Keep it warm.
  2. Mix the milk powder, flour and baking soda in a bowl. Add the ghee and rub it in.
  3. Add the milk a spoonful at a time and mix to a soft, smooth dough. Do not knead it hard. Rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Make 16 small, smooth balls with no cracks. They will double in size when fried.
  5. Heat the oil on low to medium heat. Fry the balls gently, turning often, for 8 to 10 minutes until deep golden brown. If the oil is too hot they stay raw inside.
  6. Drain them and drop them into the warm syrup. Soak for at least 1 hour.
  7. Serve warm or at room temperature.
