Instructions

  1. Add the mango cubes, milk, yogurt and honey to a blender.
  2. Add the ice cubes and cardamom powder if using.
  3. Blend on high for 45 to 60 seconds until completely smooth.
  4. Taste and add more honey if needed, then blend briefly again.
  5. Pour into glasses and serve immediately. Garnish with a few mango pieces if you like.
