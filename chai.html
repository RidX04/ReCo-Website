Instructions

  1. Boil the water with the ginger, cardamom and any other spices in a small saucepan for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the tea leaves and simmer for 1 to 2 minutes until the water turns dark.
  3. Pour in the milk, add the sugar and bring it to a boil.
  4. Lower the heat and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes. Watch it so it does not boil over.
  5. Strain into two cups and serve hot.
