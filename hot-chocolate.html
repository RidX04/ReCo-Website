Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, whisk the cocoa, sugar and salt with 1/4 cup of the milk until it forms a smooth paste.
  2. Add the rest of the milk and heat on medium-low, stirring often, until it is hot. Do not let it boil.
  3. Add the chopped chocolate and whisk until completely melted and smooth.
  4. Stir in the vanilla.
  5. Pour into two mugs and top with whipped cream or marshmallows.
