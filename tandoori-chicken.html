Tandoori Chicken

Non-Veg · Yogurt-marinated chicken roasted until charred and juicy

Instructions

  1. Make deep cuts in the chicken pieces. Rub them with the lemon juice, 1 tsp chilli powder and salt. Rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Mix the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, remaining chilli powder, garam masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, crushed kasuri methi and oil in a bowl.
  3. Coat the chicken well with this marinade. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight for the best flavour.
  4. Preheat the oven to 220°C (430°F). Place the chicken on a rack set over a tray lined with foil.
  5. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, turning once and brushing with butter halfway. The chicken is done when the juices run clear and the thickest part reaches 75°C (165°F).
  6. For extra char, grill on high for 3 to 5 minutes at the end.
  7. Serve hot with onion rings, lemon wedges and mint chutney.
