Veg Pakora

Veg · Crispy fried vegetable fritters in a spiced gram flour batter

Instructions

  1. Mix the besan, rice flour, chilli powder, turmeric, ajwain and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Add the onion, potato, spinach, green chilli and coriander. Rub them into the flour with your hands for a minute so they release some moisture.
  3. Add water a little at a time and stir to make a thick batter that coats the vegetables. Do not make it runny.
  4. Heat the oil in a deep pan on medium heat. To test it, drop in a small bit of batter: it should rise to the top in a few seconds.
  5. Drop in small spoonfuls of the mixture without crowding the pan. Fry for 4 to 5 minutes, turning, until golden and crisp.
  6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with green chutney or ketchup.
