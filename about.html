About ReCo

Cook · Enjoy

ReCo is a small recipe website made for a web design project. It currently has 16 dishes in four categories: vegetarian, non-vegetarian, dessert and drinks.

Every recipe has an ingredient checklist you can tick off while cooking, and step-by-step instructions.

What you can do on ReCo

  • Search the recipes from the home page.
  • Open a recipe by clicking its picture.
  • Tick off ingredients while you cook and watch the progress bar fill.
  • Send us feedback or a recipe suggestion through the contact form.

How to cook with ReCo

  1. Choose a category or search for a dish.
  2. Open the recipe and read the ingredient list.
  3. Tick each ingredient as you collect it.
  4. Follow the instructions from top to bottom.
  5. Enjoy your meal!
