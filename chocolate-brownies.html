Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Line an 8-inch square pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter and dark chocolate together in a bowl over simmering water, or in the microwave in short bursts. Stir until smooth and let cool a few minutes.
  3. Whisk in the sugar, then the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  4. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder and salt. Fold gently until just combined.
  5. Fold in the nuts or chocolate chips if using. Pour the batter into the pan and smooth the top.
  6. Bake for 22 to 25 minutes. A toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.
  7. Cool completely in the pan before cutting into squares.
