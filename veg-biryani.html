Veg Biryani

Veg · Fragrant layered rice with spiced vegetables

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and 1 tbsp ghee in a heavy pot. Fry the onions until deep golden brown. Take out half and set aside for layering.
  2. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chillies. Cook for 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and cook until soft.
  3. Add the vegetables, chilli powder, turmeric, biryani masala and salt. Stir in the yogurt and half the mint and coriander. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes until the vegetables are about 80% cooked.
  4. Boil the soaked rice in salted water with the whole spices until 70% cooked. Drain.
  5. Spread the rice over the vegetable masala. Top with the fried onions, remaining mint and coriander, saffron milk and the rest of the ghee.
  6. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat (dum) for 20 minutes.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes, then gently mix and serve with raita.
