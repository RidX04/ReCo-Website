Instructions

  1. Soak the sabja seeds in 1/2 cup water for 15 minutes until they swell and turn jelly-like.
  2. Boil the falooda sev in water for 3 to 5 minutes until soft. Drain and rinse under cold water.
  3. Mix the cold milk with 1 tbsp of the rose syrup.
  4. Layer each tall glass: 1 tbsp rose syrup, the sabja seeds, then the falooda sev.
  5. Pour in the rose milk and add ice cubes if you like.
  6. Top with a scoop of vanilla ice cream, the chopped nuts and tutti frutti. Serve immediately with a long spoon.
