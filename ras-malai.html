Ras Malai

Dessert · Soft milk dumplings in creamy saffron milk

Instructions

  1. Boil 1 litre of milk. Turn off the heat, add the lemon juice and stir gently until the milk curdles and the whey turns clear.
  2. Pour into a cloth-lined sieve and rinse under cold water to remove the lemony taste. Squeeze out all the water and hang for 20 minutes.
  3. Knead the chenna (milk solids) with the flour on a plate for 6 to 8 minutes until smooth and soft. Make 12 small balls and flatten them slightly.
  4. Boil the water and 3/4 cup sugar in a wide pot. Add the discs gently, cover and cook on medium heat for 15 minutes until they double in size.
  5. Meanwhile, boil the second litre of milk in another pan and simmer for 20 minutes until it reduces by about a quarter. Stir in the sugar, cardamom and saffron.
  6. Lift the cooked discs out of the syrup, gently press out the extra syrup, and add them to the thickened milk.
  7. Cool, then chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours. Top with the pistachios and almonds and serve cold.
